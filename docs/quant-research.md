# Quant research: what INVEST LL borrows from systematic funds

Quadrature Capital, one of the most profitable systematic firms in London, publishes no code and no
research. What is public about it (company accounts, SEC filings, job ads) describes how it works,
not what it trades: fully automated trading, thousands of small positions instead of a few big
bets, a team dedicated to reference data, execution treated as its own discipline, and one role
that both researches an idea and ships it.

The returns come from things a one-person project cannot copy: capital, petabytes of data, cheap
leverage from five prime brokers and low-latency execution. The **process** can be copied, and that
is what this package does. The question it answers is not "how do we earn what they earn" but "how
do we stop lying to ourselves the way a backtest on today's winners does".

## What was built

| Lesson | In INVEST LL |
|---|---|
| Reference data is a job, not an afterthought | `fetch/binance_universe.py` downloads every Binance USDT pair, the 254 delisted ones included. `quant/panel.py` splits reused tickers: `LUNAUSDT` becomes the original LUNA until its collapse and a separate coin after. |
| Only trade what you could have known about | `quant/universe.py`: each day, the 20 most liquid pairs by median volume over the previous 30 days, at least 60 days listed, no stablecoins, wrapped or leveraged tokens. Coins that later died are in it while they were liquid. |
| Many small bets, sized by risk | `quant/strategies.py`: `MomentumPortfolio` holds several coins, weights them by inverse volatility, caps each one and scales the book to a volatility target. Cash is a position. |
| Robustness over optimisation | The trend score blends 14, 30, 60 and 90 days instead of picking the best lookback. |
| Costs are part of the strategy | `quant/engine.py` fills at the next open and charges commission, slippage and square-root market impact; delisted coins are sold at their last close; Spanish tax can leave the account every July. |
| Timing luck is risk | Tranches: seven copies of a weekly rule decide on different weekdays and the account trades to their average. |
| A backtest is not evidence | `quant/validation.py`: walk-forward selection, probability of backtest overfitting (CSCV), deflated Sharpe ratio, block bootstrap. |
| Write it down before the future happens | `quant/paper.py`: append-only forward records, committed to git with their date and replayed through the same engine. |

Everything in `quant/` is pure: no network (it is in `tests/unit/test_engine_isolation.py`), no
lookahead (the strategy only receives a `MarketView` that ends at the decision day, and a test
scrambles the future to prove the decision does not change), and the engine reproduces the existing
rotation engine exactly on the four-coin case.

## Results (study of 2026-10-16)

Crypto: 1 January 2018 to 15 October 2026, from 10,000 USDT, after 0.1% commission, 0.05%
slippage and market impact on every order. The full report is generated by
`invest-ll-quant study`.

| Strategy | End value | CAGR | Sharpe | Max drawdown | Exposure | Turnover/yr |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Hold BTC | 61,324 | 22.9% | 0.65 | −81% | 100% | – |
| Hold the 20 most liquid coins (equal weight) | 2,736 | −13.7% | 0.27 | −93% | 100% | 5.4x |
| **INVEST LL's current policy** (top-1, 30 days, weekly) on the same universe | **56** | −44.6% | 0.25 | −100% | 93% | 54x |
| BTC 30-day trend (BTC or cash, weekly, 7 tranches) | 118,380 | 32.5% | 0.89 | −65% | 54% | 11x |
| Momentum portfolio, pre-registered | 46,967 | 19.2% | 0.60 | −80% | 64% | 29x |
| Stage 2: + market filter + 2% band | 266,069 | 45.2% | 1.06 | −47% | 41% | 18x |

What the numbers say:

1. **Survivorship bias was the whole story.** Without a list picked in hindsight, the liquid coins
   of each day lost 73% as a basket since 2018, and the current policy (all capital in the best
   30-day performer) went to almost zero. It chased each pump into its collapse and paid 9% a year
   in costs doing it. On BTC, ETH, SOL and BNB it looked brilliant because SOL and BNB were chosen
   knowing they would win.
2. **The pre-registered portfolio did not beat Bitcoin.** Sharpe 0.60 against 0.65, the same −80%
   drawdown. Its deflated Sharpe, after 84 trials, is 72%: compatible with luck. Only 15 of the 81
   neighbouring configurations beat Bitcoin's Sharpe, and choosing each year's best configuration
   with only the past (walk-forward) compounded at 28% a year from 2020 against 43% for holding
   Bitcoin over the same days.
3. **The trend filter on the market is what works.** Holding Bitcoin only while its 30-day return
   is positive beat holding it (Sharpe 0.89, drawdown −65%, invested 54% of the time). Applying the
   same idea to the whole portfolio (stage two) lifted it to Sharpe 1.06 with a −47% drawdown. It
   is the same finding as INVEST LL's own rotation work ("not being in when everything falls") and
   the most robust result in the trend-following literature.
4. **Stage two needs its caveats.** It was written after the first results, so its deflated Sharpe
   (97%) already counts all 84 trials. The decisive check: applied unchanged to all 81 grid
   configurations, the filter raised the Sharpe ratio of **81 of 81** (median 0.51 to 0.96) and
   made the worst fall shallower in **81 of 81** (median −80% to −50%). It is a general effect,
   not a fit to one configuration, though most of it comes from sitting out two bear markets
   (2018 and 2022). Most of its lead over the plain BTC
   trend comes from 2021, the altcoin season (+259% against +84%); in 2023 and 2024 it trailed
   both Bitcoin and the BTC trend.
5. **Timing luck is large.** Without tranches, the pre-registered portfolio ends anywhere between
   29,403 and 85,873 depending on the weekday it decides. With seven tranches it ends at 46,967.
6. **Tax is not a footnote.** Every rebalance realises gains, and tax paid each July leaves the
   account before it can compound. Holding pays once, at the end:

   | | Before tax | Tax paid every July |
   |---|---:|---:|
   | Stage 2: + market filter + band | 266,069 | 178,593 |
   | BTC 30-day trend | 118,380 | 84,203 |
   | Momentum, pre-registered | 46,967 | 27,608 |
   | Hold BTC (taxed once, selling at the end) | 61,324 | 50,660 |
7. **Capacity is small.** The same portfolio's CAGR falls from 19% with 10,000 to 11% with one
   million and below zero with ten million, as market impact on smaller coins grows.

Equities and bonds: 2 January 2008 to 15 October 2026, eight asset-class ETFs, monthly decisions in
three tranches, cash in short Treasuries.

| Strategy | CAGR | Sharpe | Max drawdown |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Hold SPY | 11.3% | 0.64 | −52% |
| 60/40 SPY/IEF | 8.3% | 0.76 | −32% |
| Equal weight, 8 ETFs | 6.0% | 0.53 | −38% |
| Risk parity | 5.4% | 0.64 | −22% |
| Risk parity + trend filter | 4.2% | 0.71 | −10% |
| Momentum top 4, 12% volatility | 5.7% | 0.72 | −16% |

No tactical portfolio beat a plain 60/40 on return per unit of risk. What they buy is a much
smaller worst case (−10% to −16% against −32%) at roughly half the return. INVEST LL's conclusion
for equities stands: contribute regularly and hold, unless avoiding deep falls matters more to you
than the return.

## What this means for using INVEST LL

- **Retire the current crypto rotation.** Top-1 rotation does not survive a universe chosen
  without hindsight.
- **Keep the market trend filter at the centre.** Either in its simplest form (BTC or cash) or in
  the stage-two portfolio. Both are under forward test from 15 October 2026.
- **Decide with the forward test, not with this backtest.** Every number above was produced with
  the past in view. The records in `paper/` were not.
- **Size the crypto sleeve for a −50% fall** even with the filter, and expect tax to take a
  visible share of any rotating strategy's gains.

## How to use it

```bash
uv run invest-ll-quant download          # every Binance USDT pair since 2017 (about 6 minutes)
uv run invest-ll-quant download --etf    # the eight asset-class ETFs plus SHY
uv run invest-ll-quant study             # everything below, into research/runs/<date>/report.html
uv run invest-ll-quant render research/runs/<folder>   # re-render a saved study
uv run invest-ll-quant paper targets --capital 2500    # what the portfolio holds now, in USDT
uv run invest-ll-quant paper record      # append today's targets to paper/momentum.jsonl
uv run invest-ll-quant paper status      # the forward result so far, against holding BTC
```

`scripts/paper-daily.ps1` runs download, record and status, then commits the record. Register it
once with the `schtasks` line in its header to run every night after the 00:00 UTC close. Every
command accepts `--json`. None of them sends orders.

## Limits

- **Binance's own selection.** The universe is what Binance listed. That is far less biased than a
  list chosen today, but a coin Binance never listed cannot appear.
- **Tax model.** FIFO per coin, the 2025 savings scale for every year, losses carried four years,
  tax paid from the account each July. USDT is treated as euros, there is no two-month rule and
  no other income. It is there to show the order of magnitude of tax drag, not to file a return.
- **Equities use US-listed ETFs** as stand-ins for the UCITS versions a Spanish investor buys.
- **Stage two** of the crypto study was written after seeing the first results. It is labelled as
  such in the report, and its deflated Sharpe counts every trial that came before it.
- **None of this predicts the future.** The forward test is the only evidence that will not have
  been fitted to the past, and it starts empty.
